Information

The Ultimate New Year Tracker follows the new year as it reaches every time zone on Earth, live. Open the Start menu (top right) to explore.

Main screen

  • Night village: a quiet hillside town with glowing windows, chimney smoke, twinkling stars and shooting stars.
  • Countdown sign: a glowing scoreboard counting down to the next time zone's midnight in days, hours, minutes and seconds, with the zone's name underneath.
  • Happy New Year! When a zone reaches midnight, the sign flashes “HAPPY NEW YEAR!” and fireworks light up the sky for a few seconds.

Start menu

  • Opens the Debug Panel, Time Zone Tracker, Live Midnight Map, Games and Party Planner. Press 1 2 3 4 5 to pick one once it's open, or use the arrow keys.
  • Use Back (top left) to return to the village from the tracker, the map, the games or the planner.

Time Zone Tracker 2

  • Next New Year card: the next zone to celebrate, with a live countdown, its local time, midnight in UTC and in your own time zone, its place in the order of arrival, and its places. When a zone rings in, the card holds on it with a celebration.
  • Next day cycle in: on the hero card and on every zone card, a live countdown to the next part of that zone's day (Night, Morning, Afternoon or Evening) and which one comes next.
  • UTC clock & progress ring: the current UTC time and how many of the 38 zones are already in the new year.
  • Day-phase bar: how many zones are in night, morning, afternoon or evening right now.
  • 38 zone cards: offset from UTC, local time and date, day phase, a mini day cycle, new-year status, midnight time, day of the year, and the countries and cities inside the zone. Zones in the new year glow gold and the next one is highlighted.
  • Search & filters: search by zone (UTC+5:30), country, city (try Toronto or Tokyo), or phase (night). Filter by All zones, In the new year, or Still waiting.

Live Midnight Map 3

  • World map: real country borders drawn in dots. Dim blue land is still in the old year, the next zone blinks between gold and blue, and solid gold land has celebrated.
  • Midnight line: a bright line sweeping west as each zone reaches midnight, with a dashed blue International Date Line.
  • Fireworks: sparks burst over each zone as it enters the new year.
  • Stat cards: zones celebrating, the next midnight and its places, and an animated days / hours / minutes / seconds countdown that flashes in the last 10 seconds.
  • Legend: a key explaining every symbol. Optional extras (a “NEW YEAR” label on the line and a ring-and-label on the next zone) can be switched on in the Debug Panel's Scene tab.

Games 4

  • Game Selection: a screen listing every game you can play. Pick a game card and press Play.
  • Trivia: a quiz of random questions (rounds of 5, 10, 20, 30, 40 or 50) about New Year traditions around the world, time zones and their offsets and abbreviations (like EST), New Year geography, calendars and fun facts. Pick a topic or play them all, answer with a click or the 1–4 keys, and read a short explanation after each question. Your best score for each topic is saved in your browser.

Party Planner 5

  • Pick your zone: choose where you're celebrating, or tap Use my device to detect it. Your choice is remembered in your browser.
  • Your midnight: a live countdown to midnight where you are, your local time, midnight in UTC, and how many zones ring in before and after you.
  • World timeline: every zone's midnight shown in your time, with a live status (celebrated X ago, or in X). Switch between 12-hour and 24-hour, or show only awake hours (6 PM to 2 AM).
  • My party list: star the zones you care about, then Copy my plan or download a .ics calendar file with a 10-minute reminder for each midnight.

Debug Panel 1

A draggable time machine for testing, in six tabs:

  • Time: pause or speed up the clock (up to 3600x or a custom speed), set an exact UTC date and time, nudge by seconds to years, and jump to the previous, next, first or last zone, or any zone you pick.
  • Fireworks: launch single bursts, rockets, or 10 and 60 second shows, and turn on party mode for the sign.
  • Scene: adjust star twinkle speed, hue and brightness, hide stars, windows, smoke or parts of the interface, and toggle the map extras.
  • Page: live frame rate and size, freeze the scene, or outline every element.
  • Site: password-protected controls to preview Site Maintenance. It locks again when the panel closes.
  • Console: run JavaScript and read the page's log.

Site maintenance

  • When switched on, the whole site is locked behind a popup with a yellow and black striped bar, a custom message, and an estimated return time. The popup never disappears by itself when that time passes.
  • The site owner controls it with the SITE_MAINTENANCE settings at the top of the HTML file.

HAPPY NEW YEAR! and the main-screen fireworks only play at normal (1x) speed. Press Esc to close menus and popups. Animations calm down if your device is set to reduce motion, and the layout adapts to phones.
